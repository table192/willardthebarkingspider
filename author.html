The author

Mario J. Pirrello

Mario J. Pirrello writes the Willard the Barking Spider books. Book One’s cover credits him as the writer. Book Two’s title page credits him for the story and the pictures.

The books

Willard the Barking Spider is the first book. Willard leaves the barn, learns to bark, and finds friends in the park. It is available now. Buy Book One on Amazon.

Willard the Barking Spider: A Festival Comes to the Park is second in the series. It is coming soon, and it does not have a purchase link yet.
