Willard the Barking Spider: A Festival Comes to the Park

Story and pictures by Mario J. Pirrello. This is a new story. Willard already has his park friends when the Friendship Festival arrives.

It is not the book about leaving the barn and learning to bark. That story is Book One.

The story

Another beautiful day in the park turns into a Friendship Festival, with tents, banners, balloons, food, music, and games. Willard, Butch, Logan, Maurizio, and Amy are there, and Amy introduces her friend Kim.

The rest of the day is in the book.

After the story there are mazes, puzzles, coloring pages, I Spy, and word matching.
