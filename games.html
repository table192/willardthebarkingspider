Games on this website

Play with Willard

Both games use Book One: the barn, the bark, the park, and the friends Willard meets there. Book Two, the festival, has its own page and is not mixed into these puzzles.

No names, no accounts, and no scores leave this page. Calm motion slows the path and turns off extra movement. It follows your system setting until you change it here.

Across the park

Willard runs a park path. Hop the logs and puddles. A miss sets him just before the obstacle so he can try that hop again. There is no game over.

Press Start, then hop.

Press Start, then hop over the logs and puddles.

Keyboard: click the game, then Space or Arrow Up hops. Touch: tap the picture or the Hop button. Holding Hop makes a gentle bounce.

Willard’s word web

A real crossword. Letters that cross have to be the same letter. Clues come from Book One, including Logan, Maurizio, and Amy in the words around the grid.

Tap a square, then type a letter.

On a phone, slide the grid sideways if a square peeks off the edge. Arrow keys move. Backspace clears a letter.
